How the judges score it ＋

Agreement starts at 100. Differences create capped penalties: temperature (20°F cap, 35% weight), precipitation probability (100-point cap, 40%), and wind (30 mph cap, 25%). If a round is unavailable, its weight is removed and the rest are renormalized. This score measures similarity, not which model is right.
